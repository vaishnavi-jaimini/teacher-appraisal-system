Teachers Appraisal Form

A simple way to run each appraisal cycle: teachers grade themselves against the school's appraisal questions, the principal reviews and grades independently, and both sides can then see the final, side-by-side result.

🍎

I'm a Teacher

Log in (or register the first time) to grade yourself and view your appraisal results.

 🗝️

I'm the Principal

Log in to manage teachers and questions, grade each teacher, and export results.

Admin login — for managing teacher accounts and appraisal questions.

Runs locally on the school network — no internet connection required.
