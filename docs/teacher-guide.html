Teacher Guide

Your Appraisal, Step by Step

Once a term, you rate your own teaching on 30 questions, and your principal rates you on the exact same 30 — separately, without seeing each other's scores first. This guide walks through logging in, completing your self-appraisal, and viewing your results once both sides are done.

Find the website address

The system runs on your school's own network — it isn't on the public internet, so it only works while you're connected to the school Wi-Fi. Ask your principal for the address and write it here:

 

Note It will look something like http://192.168.1.42:3000 — a plain address, not a normal website name. Type it into your browser exactly as given.
2

Enter your name and department

On the home page, choose I'm a Teacher. Type your Name and Department / Subject, then press Continue to my appraisal. There's no password or PIN to remember.

Tip Type your name the same way every time (e.g. always "Asha Verma," not sometimes "A. Verma"). Matching by name is how the system finds your existing appraisal again — a different spelling starts a new one.
3

Complete your self-appraisal

You'll see 30 questions grouped into 8 areas of teaching practice:

  • Lesson Planning & Preparation
  • Subject Knowledge & Delivery
  • Classroom Management
  • Student Engagement & Support
  • Assessment & Feedback
  • Communication & Collaboration
  • Professional Growth & Conduct
  • Use of Technology & Innovation

Rate yourself honestly on each, from 1 to 5:

1Needs Improvement
2Below Average
3Satisfactory
4Good
5Excellent

A bar at the top tracks how many of the 30 you've answered. There's an optional comments box at the end for anything the ratings alone don't capture. When you're done, press Save my self-appraisal.

You can reopen this page any time — just re-enter your name and department on the login screen — and change your answers. Each save replaces your previous one. Check with your principal for the cut-off date, since the system itself doesn't lock your answers.

4

See your results

Your principal rates you on the same 30 questions, independently — they don't see your self-scores while doing it, so the two views stay honest. Once they've finished, a banner appears on your appraisal page: "Your principal has completed your appraisal review." Click through to see a chart and full question-by-question comparison of your scores against theirs, along with both sets of comments.

You can view this comparison on screen, but only your principal can download or export it — so if you'd like a copy for your own records, ask them for it.

Common questions

I typed my name and it looks like a fresh, empty appraisal — did I lose my answers?

Most likely your name was typed slightly differently this time (a middle initial, extra space, different capitalization). Go back and type it exactly as you did the first time — that's what the system matches on.

Is there any password protecting my appraisal?

No — this system is built for a small, trusted staff network, and skips passwords for simplicity. Anyone who knows your name could, in principle, open your appraisal. Don't share anything in your comments you wouldn't want a colleague to stumble across.

Can my principal see my answers while I'm filling them in?

No. Self-ratings and principal ratings are kept separate until both of you have submitted — that's by design, so neither side influences the other.

I closed the browser halfway through — did I lose my answers?

No, nothing is lost until you actually press "Save." Just log back in and your last saved answers (if any) will still be there.

Does this work from home?

Only if your principal has made it reachable outside the school network. By default, it's only available on the school Wi-Fi — check with them if you're unsure.
